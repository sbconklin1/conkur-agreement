1. Parties and services

This agreement is between Conkur Media, run by Sefton Conklin ("Conkur Media"), and , represented by ("Client"). It starts on .

Each month, Conkur Media will provide:

  • 12 short-form videos, filmed in person, edited, and posted to the Client's Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube
  • 1 to 2 filming sessions per month, about 2 to 3 hours each, at a location that works for the Client, plus short voiceover recordings that can be done by phone
  • A one-time profile makeover in the first month: bio, profile photo, highlights, pinned posts and booking link
  • Booking automations, so anyone who comments a keyword or sends a DM gets the Client's booking link automatically
  • A monthly recap of views, engagement, and leads from social

Paid ads: if the Client wants to boost a post, ad spend is paid by the Client directly to the ad platform.

2. Fees and payment

  • Monthly fee: $, due on the 1st of each month, paid in advance for that month.

3. Term and cancellation

  • This agreement is month to month. There is no long-term commitment.
  • Either side can cancel at any time by text or email. To avoid being billed for the next month, cancel before the 1st of that month.
  • Payments already made are not refunded, but no further payments are owed after cancellation.

4. Client responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  • Be available for 1 to 2 filming sessions per month, about 2 to 3 hours each, and record short voiceovers when asked
  • Respond to leads within 1 hour during business hours (DMs, calls and booking requests from social)
  • Provide login access or admin access to the social accounts being managed
  • Review and approve anything that needs approval (like pricing or offers shown in videos) within 48 hours
  • Make sure anyone filmed at the business, including staff and customers, is okay being on camera

If a filming session is missed or canceled with less than 24 hours' notice, that month's videos may be reduced.

5. Results guarantee

If the Client does not get at least new leads from social media within the first 90 days in a row of service, Conkur Media will keep providing the services in Section 1 at no charge, for up to 2 additional months, until the Client reaches that number.

During free months, the Client pays nothing. Regular billing resumes on the 1st of the month after the number is reached. There is no back pay for free months. If the number still isn't reached after 2 free months, the Client may cancel with nothing further owed.

What counts as a lead: a new person who reaches out through social media asking about the Client's services. That includes a DM, a comment with the booking keyword, a booking request through the profile link, or a call where they say they found the Client on social. Leads are tracked through the booking automations and the DM inbox.

The guarantee only applies if the Client:

  • Stays with Conkur Media for the first 90 days in a row, without canceling or pausing
  • Keeps all payments current
  • Shows up for scheduled filming sessions and voiceover recordings
  • Responds to leads within 1 hour during business hours, as listed in Section 4

The guarantee covers new leads only. Conkur Media does not promise a specific number of views, followers, sales, or revenue.

6. Content ownership and other terms

  • Ownership: once paid for, the Client owns all final videos and can keep using them after the agreement ends.
  • Portfolio use: Conkur Media may share the Client's videos and results in its own portfolio and marketing, unless the Client asks in writing not to.
  • Account access: the Client keeps full ownership of all social accounts. When the agreement ends, Conkur Media will return or remove its access.
  • Platform changes: Conkur Media isn't responsible for changes to social media algorithms, account restrictions, or outages outside its control.
  • Confidentiality: both sides will keep each other's business details, logins and pricing private.
  • Independent contractor: Conkur Media is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Client.
  • Governing law: this agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
